The short version of everything people ask us about HarperLinks.

It gives your business an AI workforce in one dashboard: an AI team that learns your business from your website, writes and schedules your social content, captures and follows up leads in a CRM, books clash-checked appointments, and runs automations — with a daily report telling you what happened and what needs attention.

Yes, once your accounts are linked during setup — the scheduler fires posts exactly on time. Until a platform is connected, posts run in preview mode: they're written and scheduled for real, but not sent. The dashboard always shows which is which. We'll never tell you something was posted when it wasn't.

Total control. Every AI employee has three modes — Assisted (drafts and asks you), Supervised (handles routine work, escalates big calls) and Autonomous (runs only the workflows you've approved). Sensitive actions always need your sign-off, and everything is logged.

No. You paste your website address and chat in plain English — "write me posts about our spring special", "book Jane in for Tuesday 2pm". The AI handles the strategy, writing and timing.

Working now: learning your business from your website, writing and scheduling social content, branded images and videos, your CRM and lead capture, clash-checked appointment booking, automation workflows, a built-and-published website, and your daily AI report.

In development: the AI phone receptionist, SMS, email sending and social DMs. We mark these clearly everywhere rather than quietly implying they're live — if a feature isn't connected, your dashboard says so.

Credits are how we meter the AI work. Every task uses a set number — a social post is 5, a lead captured is 1, a support reply is 2, a full website build is 1,500. Your plan includes a monthly allowance, and the dashboard shows exactly what's left and where it went.

The important work keeps running. Lead capture, follow-ups, bookings and support replies carry on using a small emergency buffer, so you never lose an enquiry because of a number. Content, images, video and website edits pause until you top up — and we warn you before you get there, not after.

On Professional and Business, unused credits stay for an extra month. On Enterprise, two. Starter resets each cycle. Credits you buy as a top-up never expire while your plan is active, and the oldest ones are always spent first.
