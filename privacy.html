Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026 · HarperLinks, Perth, Western Australia

HarperLinks is a father-and-son business. We treat your data the way we'd want ours treated. This page says what we collect and why, without the legal fog.

What we collect

  • When you sign up or enquire: your name, email, phone and business details — so we can build your account and contact you.
  • When you use your dashboard: the content you create (posts, leads, bookings, website drafts) is stored in your own isolated account. No other customer can see it, ever.
  • When you chat with our site assistant: the conversation, so it can answer you. Don't paste passwords or card numbers into any chat.
  • On this website: anonymous page counts only — no cookies, no IP addresses, no tracking across the web. That's why there's no cookie banner.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data. To anyone. Ever.
  • We never post to your social accounts without your setup and say-so.
  • We never show your information to other customers — every account's data is physically separated.

Who touches your data

To run the service we use trusted providers: Anthropic (the AI that writes your content), our publishing pipeline for social posting, Stripe (payments — we never see your card number), and our hosting providers. Each sees only what it needs to do its job.

AI, honestly

Your AI team writes content based on what it learns from your website and knowledge base. Our team can access your account to set things up and help — every such access is logged in your own activity feed where you can see it.

Your rights

Email us and we'll show you what we hold, fix it, export it, or delete your account and everything in it. Australian Privacy Principles apply.

Talk to a human

Questions? Email harperlinksteam@gmail.com — it goes straight to us, not a ticket queue.
