NOTE ON BACKGROUNDS
The logo is a neon glow, which is light. It looks its best on dark. On a white
background the shape still reads fine but the halo turns into a soft grey haze —
that's physics, not a file problem. For anything light, use the logo shape
without relying on the glow.
